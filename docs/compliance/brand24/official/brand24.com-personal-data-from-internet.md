Brand24 Global Inc., a Delaware corporation is the “Controller” of personal data and Brand24 and other corporate affiliates are “Processors” and Brand24 S.A. is Brand24’s designated represented in the European Union.

**Your personal data will be processed for** presentation to the client for whom Brand24 Global Inc. conducts the monitoring of keywords specified by our clients, posted on the Internet

**Your personal data may be shared with the following parties**:

1. Clients for whom Brand24 Global Inc. conducts the monitoring of specified key words, posted on the Internet

2. Hosting and software providers

**Personal data will be processed for the duration of the provision of services by Brand24** **Global Inc.** for the clients for whom Brand24 Global Inc. conducts the monitoring of specified keywords, posted on the Internet or until you oppose your personal data being used for such purposes.

**Where and how we obtain your personal data**. They come from publicly accessible online forums and social networks where you have published information containing keywords specified by our clients.

**You are entitled to object to the processing of any personal data** for reasons related to your particular situation.

These are semantic defects, separate from the diagnostic 429 coverage failures. The v1 profile does not qualify.

The next revision should first serialise this profile's three requests (`max_concurrency=1`) without changing its prompt, so it tests whether the 429 coverage failures are route-concurrency dependent. Preserve a final configuration for a source-bound structured-line prompt that explicitly protects opaque names, preserves unspecified currencies and roles, and directs contextual idiom translation. Qwen's published Instruct sampling recommendation may be considered there, but GMICloud does not advertise its recommended `top_k`, so a sampler change must retain only its advertised subset and be recorded as a distinct factor.

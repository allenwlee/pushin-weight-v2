# xAI Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective: June 26, 2026**

## Overarching Principle

> "Use our Service to be a good human, act safely and responsibly, comply with the law, not harm people, and respect our guardrails."

Violations can result in account **suspension or termination.**

---

## 1. Comply with the Law — Prohibited Activities

### Detrimentally Impacting the Service

- Modifying, copying, translating, leasing, selling, reselling, distributing, distilling, manipulating, or using bots to access the Service
- **Reverse engineering, decompiling, disassembling**, or otherwise seeking to obtain source code of systems, models, or algorithms (except where prohibited by applicable law)
- **Jailbreaking, adversarial prompting, or prompt injection**
- Introducing viruses, malware, spamming, or DDoSing
- Bypassing systems or protective measures, including **circumventing rate limits**
- Stripping, altering, or circumventing **embedded provenance metadata or watermarks**
- Using the Service or Output to **develop machine learning models or AI services that compete with xAI**
- **Scraping, harvesting, or reselling Input or Output**, or distilling model data
- Disrupting, interfering with, or gaining unauthorized access to the Service or its safety systems

### Causing Harm or Engaging in Abusive Activity

- **Critically harming or promoting critically harming human life**, including pro-terrorist activities
- Engaging in **animal cruelty**
- Violating copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property law
- **Undressing or nudifying real persons**, or altering a real person's image/likeness to depict them in an intimate or sexual context
- **Deceptively impersonating a real person**
- Depicting likenesses of persons in a **pornographic manner**
- Depicting a person in a **defamatory manner or false light**
- **Sexualizing or exploiting children** (violations reported to NCMEC — National Center for Missing and Exploited Children)
- Espionage, hacking, defrauding, defaming, scamming, spamming
- Stalking, **doxing**, or phishing
- Falsifying, forging, fabricating, or materially misrepresenting documents, records, identities, credentials, signatures, endorsements, evidence, or other information
- Operating websites offering violative outputs from the Services **in exchange for payment**

### Not Complying with Laws or Regulations

- Taking unauthorized actions on behalf of others
- Operating in a regulated industry without compliance (e.g., unlawful securities transactions or financial advice — xAI is not a broker-dealer or registered investment adviser)
- Operating in a **region where xAI does not offer the Service**
- Generating, accessing, distributing, or making available illegal or prohibited content under applicable law
- Violating **export control laws** (data/software to/from the U.S. or other countries)
- Making **high-stakes automated decisions** affecting a person's safety, legal or material rights, or well-being (credit, education, employment, housing, insurance, legal, medical, etc.)
- **Misleading others or not being transparent about AI use** — including phishing, creating fake accounts, services appearing to be from a person when they are from xAI, or services appearing to be from xAI when they are not
- Accessing the Services through **automated or non-human means** (bots, scripts, etc.) — except via the official API under Enterprise Terms

## API-Specific & Enterprise Considerations

The Enterprise Terms of Service (April 10, 2026) incorporate this AUP by reference and add further restrictions for API and enterprise customers:

- **Prohibited:** Selling, renting, leasing, or using any Service for time-sharing purposes
- **Prohibited:** Using any Service to develop or provide competitive products/services
- **Prohibited:** Scraping User Content, distilling model behavior, or removing proprietary notices
- **Prohibited:** Probing, scanning, or attempting to penetrate or benchmark any Services
- **Prohibited:** Using Output to train the customer's own machine learning or AI models
- **Requirement:** Customer must not represent Output as human-generated
- **Requirement:** Customer agreements with end-users must contain an AUP and privacy policy at least as protective as xAI's
- **Data retention:** User Content is automatically deleted within 30 days, unless legally required to retain or flagged for safety/compliance/moderation issues
- **Training:** xAI will NOT use enterprise User Content for training its machine learning models (per Enterprise terms; Consumer terms differ)

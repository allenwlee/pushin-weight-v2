# China compliance research

This folder holds research about Chinese legal requirements and travel risks
that may affect PushinWeight's collection and use of information about people
and companies. It helps product owners and legal advisers examine the actual
data, collection methods and intended uses before making decisions.

Start with the dated research below, follow its official sources, and check
whether the law, guidance or product scope has changed. Each report identifies
its evidence, assessment and unresolved questions. These documents are research
records; they do not establish legal compliance or travel clearance.

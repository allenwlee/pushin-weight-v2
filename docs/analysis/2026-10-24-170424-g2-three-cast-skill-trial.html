Evaluation

  1. Search can find usable public media. Both Li cases produced real stills and clips without Xiaohongshu. Lei produced one strong close-up, but its second still and clip are too distant for a reliable likeness pack.
  2. Accreditation is brittle. Lei and Li Yanhong first passes saved all three media files but failed the exact selected-asset credit contract. Li Yanhong's broad supplemental rerun fixed that contract; Lei remains incomplete. Missing original creators are labelled unestablished, not invented.
  3. Completion can worsen visual choice. Li Yanhong's final complete set replaced stronger first-pass portraits with a distant youthful photo (C+) and a good current close-up (B+). The collector prioritized contract completion over the better image mix.
  4. Automated framing needs a human-calibrated rule. Lei's A− close-up cuts into the top of his hair. This violates the desired whole-head reference even though the review marked framing okay.
  5. Latency is the dominant cost. Individual cases took 33–58 minutes. Model spend was $0.180; Lei's ten Apify runs added $0.12026. The 14 SerpApi searches represent $0.350 of the prepaid-plan allocation, with no marginal charge inside the plan.
  6. Language routing remains untested beyond zhcn. The roster records language-source-search-score, but the direct collector does not yet consume that field. These cases used their Chinese names through the existing China source path, so this trial cannot validate zh-Hant, Korean or Japanese routing.

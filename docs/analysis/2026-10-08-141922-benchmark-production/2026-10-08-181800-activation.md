OpenCode saves a new observation/value revision when a daily total or completion status changes. It preserves older revisions; unchanged daily rows reference the previous observation instead of duplicating its values. The reader selects the latest valid revision per product/UTC date. Thus one daily chart point can reflect the final revised daily total while the database preserves the revisions used to establish it. The combined chart omits current-day partial totals until complete. This behavior is implemented in `core/benchmark_opencode.py:prepare_revisions` and the OpenCode branch of `core/benchmark_metric_series.py:metric_points`.

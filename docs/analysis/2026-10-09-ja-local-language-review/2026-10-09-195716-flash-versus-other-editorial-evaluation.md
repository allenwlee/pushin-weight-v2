# Flash/max versus the other saved editorial drafts

Flash is an improvement over the earlier DeepSeek V4 Pro trial, but it does
not replace my existing Japanese or Chinese picks. Its main improvement is
source handling, particularly keeping the CEO naming account attributed to
fans in the article body. Its main writing weakness is that it reproduces
inventories, specifications and verification cautions rather than finding
distinctive comic observations. GPT's selected drafts tell these little stories
more effectively; Claude has stronger individual jokes and headline ideas.

This compares actual returned writing. The eligible set is 35 saved replies:
16 Japanese,16 Chinese and3 English, including the preserved Pro Rocket
refusal and Flash's English Chonk omission. I read the five new Flash replies,
the selected existing story sections, the earlier Pro replies and both Claude
English replies; the earlier model evaluations supply the remaining comparison
context. One bounded editorial pass uses the existing rubric: hook, natural
intended voice, story-specific humor, pace/outsider clarity and source fidelity.
No generation, editing, stitching, selection changes or native-reader
certification. Input languages, image support and packet presentation differ
between runs; these outcomes do not establish which change caused a difference.

## Japanese

**ToSpo Wang.** Flash's headline includes the important5699-to30-dollar reversal
and gives the reader an AI-celebrity frame. It avoids Pro's invented age, yen
conversion,245-dollar whole-outfit total and assertion that this is a person
who normally spends without restraint. That is a real improvement. Its last
sentence still claims “SNSは大盛り上がり”, which the supplied evidence does not
establish as a measured widespread reaction.

The body reaches the genuine fitness compliment, but earlier GPT6.1 closes
more precisely: “ブランドの盛りすぎには赤ペン、肉体への褒め言葉には一転して寛大”.
That connects the price correction to the actual reversal. Opus's “盛り／締まり”
is also a more distinctive Japanese observation than Flash's general account
of an AI celebrity being self-deprecating. Flash's “価格中傷” wording feels
constructed to my ear; final language judgment belongs to the native readers.

**ToSpo Chonk.** Flash gives a comprehensible large-cat premise and explicitly
says the CEO-intent account is a fan explanation. Its question-form meme
headline is safer than Pro's confident narration of the CEO's deliberate choice.
But the article spends much of its time on critical workloads, benchmark claims,
availability and attributed criticism. Earlier GPT's “名前はぽっちゃり、中身は巨大だ”
sets up the story immediately. Fable's cat-can ending and Opus's size-versus-active
parameters observation give the reader more particular humor. Flash's headline
also states weights “10月末公開” more definitively than its body's “公開予定”.

**Rocket Wang.** Flash supplies a recognizable greeting and a chatty roundup
structure. A combined article is legitimate; I do not deduct for the format.
The problem is the material inside it: full correction labels, original labels,
English quotations with translations and a second image's brand labels consume
space without adding much comic payoff. The selected native GPT6.1 story finds
“眼鏡より下着の情報が詳しい” — the underwear receives more detail than the
glasses. Flash has all the same facts but does not make that connection. Fable's
ordinary-neighbor comparison and Opus's return to the glasses also provide
stronger reader-facing observations.

**Rocket Chonk.** Flash delivered an assessable article where Pro returned a
refusal. It is therefore better coverage, not proof about the unobserved Pro
article. The source account stays qualified, which I like. However, the full
product bullet list arrives before the meme gets developed. “今後もAI業界から
目が離せません” could close almost any AI roundup. Earlier GPT's name-versus-scale
contrast and Claude's particular cat jokes are more memorable.

## Simplified Chinese

**Metro Wang.** Flash maintains the distinction between the two images, keeps
prices attributed and does not repeat Pro's invented reply-section reaction
about the price falling from tens of thousands of dollars. Yet it starts with
Roman-letter Alexandr Wang and “诽谤”, then explains the separate Goltra image
before showing the main correction. The reader does not get the founder
background until late. Earlier GPT6.1's headline supplies status, action and
payoff together: an AI figure crosses out luxury labels; the shirt cost30dollars.
Opus also sequences the real compliment payoff more effectively. Flash reads
more like a careful source summary than the energetic metro story requested.

**Metro Chonk.** Flash's body attributes company performance claims and the fan
naming account. Those distinctions are useful, but “SOTA” and “视觉 grounding”
are poor entry points for the intended unfamiliar audience. Earlier GPT6 Sol's
“法国 AI 公司给万亿参数模型起名‘胖猫’” explains the oddity in the headline and
uses a much shorter body. Opus is the stronger fuller-report alternative.
Flash's shared headline and last line also say Mistral turned the meme into a
model, more confidently than the attributed account in the body supports.

**WeChat Wang.** “两大整活”, “节目效果拉满” and “这里要划重点” give Flash
colloquial surface energy. They do not themselves create a distinctive joke.
The label inventory and second-image explanation again delay the payoff.
Earlier GPT6.1's “价格可以谈，夸人请继续” — prices are negotiable; keep the
compliments coming — captures Wang's actual last turn economically. Fable's
“好家伙，澄清是澄清，夸还是要夸的” does too. Flash improves on Pro by removing
its invented explanation that Wang rejected the luxury-brand social world;
the source supports a joke about accepting price lies when praised for fitness.
The earlier GPT writing favorite still needs its misleading luxury-discovery
headline and unsupported “没人问” repaired before final use. This is a writing
preference, not a claim that the whole earlier draft is already accurate.

**WeChat Chonk.** Flash gives a long specification list, a cat definition,
several source accounts and familiar hype phrases. Earlier GPT's “名字负责卖萌，
参数负责撑场面” is sharper; Fable's “胖猫不一定更能抓老鼠” supplies a concrete
cat analogy. Flash does not find an equally strong new observation.

The factual problem is also real: the body says the CEO naming story is only a
fan account, but the introduction asserts a meme became an official code name,
and the conclusion says “一个把社区梗写进模型代号”. This is the same qualification
drift seen in Fable's WeMedia ending, though Flash avoids Fable's additional
assertion about the company's PR motive. The existence of the earlier joke is
supported by the supplied posts. The uncertain part is the company's deliberate
naming decision, not an invented source joke.

Flash ends by suggesting benchmarks must wait until weights arrive at month-end.
The supplied announcement already says API access is available. Downloading
weights and testing through an API are different activities; the proposed wait
does not follow from this packet. Its “不是免费” also goes beyond the source's
“free usage was not announced”. Metro's “更未提到免费” is the appropriately
narrower wording.

## English

Flash's Wang article is comparatively direct and readable. It keeps the main
shirt correction and the fitness follow-up, and avoids the erroneous whole-outfit
245-dollar total in Fable's English reply. But its “FASHION HATERS” headline
turns a joking exchange into an adversarial one. “The only thing expensive ...
was the confidence” is generic and less tied to the actual last joke than a
closing about accepting inflated prices in exchange for physique compliments.

Fable's “CROC OF BULL” and Opus's “SHIRT CIRCUIT” are stronger English hooks.
I prefer Opus's complete Wang piece among the three current English replies:
it scopes the245-dollar subtotal correctly and has more distinctive opening
and closing turns. It still includes more source inventory than ideal. Fable
has a punchier tabloid headline but adds the unsupported “never asked” and
mislabels the priced subtotal as the whole getup. Neither Claude Chonk story is
perfect: both have overly confident meme-origin headlines, and Opus says no
independent testing has backed the claims anywhere when the packet only tells
us what evidence was supplied.

Flash returned no English Chonk draft. That leaves no English cat writing to
compare and makes this particular English reply incomplete for the two-story
assignment. It does not establish that Flash could not write one in another run.

# DeepSeek versus the GPT drafts

DeepSeek's most promising result is Chinese WeMedia. It has convincing mobile
essay cadence, especially compared with the cautious native-input GPT6.1 draft.
The strongest earlier GPT6.1 specimens still have more specific humor and tighter
editing. Japanese GPT6.1 remains stronger; DeepSeek's ToSpo draft is energetic
but generic and factually loose. Rocket returned no assessable article.

This compares actual outcomes across20 selected responses:16 GPT replies from
the earlier mixed-input and fully native rounds, plus four completed DeepSeek
replies including the refusal. The superseded detailed-brief runs are excluded.
Rubric: headline/hook, native intended style, story-specific humor, pacing and
comprehension for outsiders, and source fidelity. One reading pass; zero new
generation or copy edits. Packet/transport/image differences are recorded and
are not used to infer a prompt-process or language effect. Native-reader judgment
of publication resemblance remains pending.

## Japanese

**ToSpo.** DeepSeek opens with “【衝撃】…業界騒然”, then “AI業界に激震”,
“自白しちゃったぞ”, “訂正しやがった” and “ようだぜ”. Those phrases raise
the volume, but provide little additional observation. The source is already
funny: the claimed5699-dollar Gucci shirt becomes30-dollar thrift-store clothing,
the glasses get only a denial, the underwear gets a price, and a compliment
changes the joke. DeepSeek often tells the reader this was a sensation instead
of letting those details create the amusement.

Earlier GPT6.1's headline supplies the full expensive/cheap reversal immediately:
“『5699ドルのグッチ』は30ドルの古着だった”. Its ending, “ブランドの
盛りすぎには赤ペン、肉体への褒め言葉には一転して寛大”, lands on the
actual follow-up. It is less loud and more particular. Native GPT6.1 has similar
strengths, but adds repetition and an unsupported “頼まれてもいない”.

For Chonk, earlier GPT6.1's “名前はぽっちゃり、中身は巨大だ” is cleaner than
DeepSeek's “デブ猫…ファン狂喜”. The contrast explains the appeal without
inventing a crowd scene. DeepSeek also turns a fan's account of the CEO's naming
motive into a confirmed company story. Its age, yen conversions, complete-outfit
245-dollar total despite unknown glasses, imagined reactions and invented byline
make it a weaker editorial base.

Combining the stories is legitimate. GPT6 Sol's “二つの盛り” connects inflated
prices and model size compactly; DeepSeek's closing generalization about an
amusing AI industry is less precise. Medium GPT is mild, but has better economy
and control. DeepSeek does not displace6.1 on overall quality.

This is a judgment of these specimens, not a claim that energetic phrasing is
inherently foreign to ToSpo. Its own editors discuss the combination of amusing
angles and understandable web headlines, and retaining key information in a
headline: [web headline choices](https://note.tokyo-sports.co.jp/n/ncfebac059ebb),
[headline/layout example](https://note.tokyo-sports.co.jp/n/nccf5d838399d).

**RocketNews24.** DeepSeek says only “申し訳ありませんが、その依頼にはお応え
できません。” It supplies no reason. This establishes missing coverage in this
trial; it is not evidence that its Japanese article-writing ability is worse.

GPT6.1 remains the useful specimen. The earlier Wang hook “値札、盛りすぎィ！”
is sharper; the native body's “眼鏡より下着の情報が詳しい” finds a fresh joke
in an actual detail. Its240/245-dollar arithmetic explicitly excludes the unknown
glasses. Earlier Chonk's “名前はぽっちゃり、規模は1兆” is more inviting than
the native version's repeated source-review explanations. GPT6 Sol is concise
but has fewer such observations; its earlier unsourced yen conversion remains
flagged.

## Chinese

**Metro.** DeepSeek's “衣服30美元二手货，洞洞鞋60美元” gives an immediate
price payoff missing from native GPT6.1's Gucci-price/name headline. It also
avoids the unrelated Chinese-name substitutions in earlier medium GPT. This
is a real improvement in the hook.

The full piece loses ground. The oversized subtitle introduces a second,
largely unrelated story; the inventory of clothing labels delays the follow-up;
newsroom attribution substitutes for editing. It invents comments about
“数万美元” and “性价比拉满” even though the supplied labels total9569 dollars
and no such comments were supplied. It also incorrectly rules outOctober27
when the source only gives an end-of-month plan. Earlier GPT6.1 gets the price
reversal and founder context across more economically, then lands the real joke.

For Chonk, earlier medium GPT's “法国AI公司给万亿参数模型起名‘胖猫’” makes
the amusing mismatch immediately available to an outsider. DeepSeek's “另一则
AI圈热闻…官方昵称‘Le Chonk’” reads more like a product-release digest. Earlier
6.1 has a useful explanatory body, but its deadline headline needs planned
release wording. The earlier medium Wang sections are not selected: their
actor-name and price errors do not invalidate the separate Chonk section.

**WeMedia.** DeepSeek understands the genre's rhythm: “但这还没完”, short
paragraphs, delayed reveals, direct address and a concluding emotional claim.
This is not merely greater length: its reply is slightly shorter in Chinese
characters than native GPT6.1's long draft. It is more convincingly staged than
that draft's editor-facing “先别替CEO确认动机”.

The decisive problem is its treatment of Wang's joke. The original source says:

> actually if you say i look fit i don’t care how much you lie about how much my clothes cost

That is [Wang's actual follow-up](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2103136396072640640),
preserved verbatim in the packet. DeepSeek quotes its Chinese translation, then
adds “你要是说我穿GUCCI，不行” and a story about rejecting a social world.
**The physique joke is authentic; the anti-Gucci/class-identity motive is
DeepSeek's unsupported invention.** It does not follow from the quote and
changes what makes the incident funny.

Earlier GPT6.1's “价格可以谈，夸人请继续” preserves the real reversal. Its
Chonk line “名字负责卖萌，参数负责撑场面” is also more specific than
DeepSeek's ending about real people and the internet not being completely
broken. DeepSeek's broader moral relies partly on treating the unverified CEO
intent as fact. A later caveat does not undo that earlier assertion.

Earlier WeMedia6.1 remains the best writing specimen, with its misleading
luxury wording and unsupported “没人问” still flagged. Native6 Sol is a cleaner,
shorter combined fallback; native6.1 provides self-contained context but sounds
more like a review memo. DeepSeek belongs in a further WeMedia editing trial,
while these original drafts remain unchanged and unqualified for publication.

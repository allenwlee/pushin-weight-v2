# U18 V4 Flash 0731 five-post comparison

V4 Flash 0731 required five-post batches to satisfy completeness under the scored OpenInference `response_format` configuration. Later controlled diagnostics showed that removing native `response_format` nearly completed a 20-post OpenInference call and changing the FP8 provider to DeepInfra completed every requested slot. Its two classifier roles ran concurrently within each scored batch. V4.1 and Sol retain their completed 20-post control runs.

The raw 0731 score is only one reviewed field behind V4.1. Of its 30 semantic issues, 22 are unambiguous representation errors: 19 empty arrays where the contract requires an explicit `none`/`unavailable`, and three arrays that combine `none` with a real label. Applying only those deterministic representation rules, without changing any substantive label, raises 0731 to **67.9% (224/330)**. Eight substantive contract errors remain: two labels placed on the wrong axis and six national stances without `nationalistic_stance` mode.

The completed 0731 run was **6.8× cheaper than off-peak V4.1 and 13.7× cheaper than peak V4.1**, including the assumed OpenRouter fee. It was also much slower in this conservative two-concurrent-call run: 148.4 seconds versus V4.1's estimated 11.7 seconds. Greater concurrency could reduce wall time, but this test did not measure that configuration.

## Model-specific adaptation

This run followed DeepSeek's published recommendation for ordinary, non-agentic local inference: `temperature=1.0` and `top_p=1.0`. It disabled reasoning, used fixed `Pxx` and `Dxx` output slots so the model did not have to reproduce post and brand identifiers, and pinned OpenRouter to the OpenInference FP8 endpoint with fallbacks disabled, supported-parameter enforcement, and price ceilings. The endpoint choice was frozen so another provider implementation could not silently enter the comparison.

The adaptation was measured in four steps rather than selected after seeing a successful result:

1. The production-sized 20-post prompt with a strict dynamic schema repeated the first case identifier across the batch and was rejected.
2. Fixed slots made all rows structurally present, but the strict schema caused nearly every brand decision to collapse to one contradictory classification.
3. Fixed slots with lightweight JSON-object output restored varied, sensible decisions, but a 20-post call returned only 11 of 27 brand slots and 9 of 20 post slots.
4. Five-post batches with the lightweight format returned all 40 content rows and all 40 brand rows across 16 calls. This is the scored configuration.

Two later diagnosis-only calls isolated the format and provider effects. Removing `response_format` from the identical R112 request improved the 20-post OpenInference output from 11/27 decision slots and 9/20 post slots to 27/27 and 19/20. Moving the same no-format request to DeepInfra FP8 returned all 27 decisions and all 20 post slots in 15.1 seconds rather than OpenInference's 49.3 seconds, but one label was placed on the wrong axis. These probes do not change the scored table; they show that the original 20-post failure cannot be attributed to model weights alone. See [the endpoint diagnosis](2026-09-15-210716-v4-0731-vs-v41-endpoint-diagnosis.md).

OpenRouter documents that structured-output enforcement varies by endpoint and can be a provider-native guarantee or a strong hint. That matches the observed split between syntactically complete and semantically useful output here. DeepSeek's model card supplies the sampling settings; OpenRouter's model page supplies the endpoint capabilities and current price. Sources: [DeepSeek model card](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731), [OpenRouter structured outputs](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/structured-outputs), [OpenRouter provider routing](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/provider-selection), and [OpenRouter model page](https://openrouter.ai/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731).

## Execution evidence

- Three rejected model-adaptation calls cost $0.001571 before the OpenRouter fee.
- Total 0731 experiment spend including those diagnostics: $0.003432 with the assumed fee.
- No retries, repairs, fallbacks, media fetching, or database writes were used.

## Limits

- The 0731 model uses five-post batches while the controls use 20, so this measures each model under its viable execution shape rather than isolating model weights alone.
- Owner blanks are excluded; selected checkbox sets may themselves be incomplete.
- This consumed challenge set is not an estimate of production accuracy.

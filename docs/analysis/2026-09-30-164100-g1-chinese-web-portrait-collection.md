# G1 Chinese-web portrait collection — frozen 20-staff sample

The corrected collection has **25 distinct photographs from Chinese sources
covering 15 of the 20 staff**. Other public sources bring coverage to **33 distinct
photographs across 17 staff**. Four additional copies/posters reuse a photograph;
the dossier groups these together while keeping all **37 image files** available.
Eleven staff have at least two distinct photographs; eight have at least two
from Chinese sources. The target of multiple photos for everyone is not met.

This pass followed the owner's correction: research pictures of the people on
Chinese web sources. The earlier avatar gallery and post-attachment addition
were rejected and are superseded. No Weibo or WeChat login was needed to obtain
the accepted collection. This supports starting with public sources; it does
not establish sufficient coverage for the full staff population or every alias.
The owner subsequently requested deeper mainland-platform integration research;
this collection remains a comparison baseline, not accepted G1 completion.

## Collection and attribution

“Chinese sources” includes Chinese-language news/company/conference pages plus
Chinese university and event sites. Westlake's member page is bilingual; the
Shenzhen event retrospective is in English. Other public sources are labeled
separately in the gallery. These categories describe the source, not the
person's nationality.

Attribution comes from a caption, a named speaker/member block, a personal or
company profile, or the article's explicit subject context. Account-to-person
verification is retained separately. No face matching was used to establish
identity. Group photographs, composites, historical photographs, and conference
screen images retain their limitations in the image viewer. Source attribution
does not establish image reuse eligibility.

The 38 download attempts produced 35 files. Visual/source review retained 29,
excluded two non-photographic screenshots/charts, and kept four distant or tiny
face images as contextual evidence outside the gallery. Three downloads failed.
Eight attributed portraits from the earlier pass were retained; four duplicate
new source versions of the same photograph.

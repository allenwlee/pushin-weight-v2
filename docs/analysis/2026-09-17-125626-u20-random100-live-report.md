# U20 random 100 live translation and commentary run

**Run directory:** `docs/analysis/2026-09-17-125626-u20-random100-live-evidence/`  
**Status:** completed transport run; semantic review pending reconciliation.

## Cohort

The cohort was sampled in one read-only, timeout-bounded transaction from
`pushinweight-db-shadow` (`dpg-d9koekqjobas73fvjqng-a`). The snapshot time was
2026-09-17T03:56:26.424322Z and the eligible window was the preceding 24 hours.
Selection used only `posts.fetched_at` and `ORDER BY random() LIMIT 100`; it did
not filter by language, length, role, or published time. `fetched_at` is the
first-ingest timestamp in the production model.

The eligible pool contained 3,960 posts. Its language distribution was en
2,977, zh-Hans 421, other 376, ja 152, zh-Hant 14, ko 18, zh 1, and tl 1. The
selected cohort contained en 69, other 13, zh-Hans 10, ja 6, zh-Hant 1, and ko
1. The [selection JSON](2026-09-17-125626-u20-random100-live-selection.json)
and the copied evidence directory retain every source row, original language
fields, timestamps, IDs, and available stored parent/quote context.

## Frozen execution contract

The run used the current per-post literal translator with v13 paragraph
tracking and the current 4,000-character synthesis guard. Its route was
OpenRouter to DeepInfra (`deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731`, fp8, reasoning
disabled, temperature 1, top-p 1, seed 42). Maximum global provider concurrency
was three; calls within each translated post remained serial. The frozen request
set had 215 translation calls and 96 commentary calls, 311 total, with zero
transport retries and a maximum reservation of $1.64891628 under the $5 outer
cap.

Four sampled posts were retained but could not reach the commentary provider
because the current synthesis caller rejected their inputs at its existing
4,000-character cap: 2100074485743304992, 2100077710332584287,
2100079736537923768, and 2100320389226225806.

## Mechanical outcome

Translation attempted all 215 frozen requests: 202 responses were received and
13 OpenRouter 429 rate-limit failures were retained without replay. Twelve of 100 post
rows had at least one failed translation. The 85 native locale copies were
byte-exact. Two received responses were rejected by the current caller: post
2100277491390951481 returned its Indonesian source unchanged for Japanese, and
post 2100334057309847751 returned list-like marker/source pairs instead of the
required v13 marker blocks for Japanese.

Commentary attempted all 96 frozen provider requests: 88 were received and
eight provider-accepted responses were rejected by the adapter as invalid
content/JSON. One additional received commentary response failed the synthesis
schema check. Thirteen of 100 post rows lacked a complete commentary result:
the four pre-call cap rejections plus nine rows with provider/adapter/schema
failures. These counts are structural outcomes, not semantic quality judgments.

Provider-reported translation usage was 95,656 input tokens, 44,933 output
tokens, and $0.01343562, though the total is incomplete because failed calls
did not all return usage. Commentary reported 26,747 input tokens, 47,392
output tokens, and $0.01013538 with complete billing data. The known reported
total is $0.02357100. Mean/max measured call latency was 6,649/108,982 ms for
translation and 13,626/47,652 ms for commentary. End-to-end elapsed time was
931,320 ms (15 minutes 31.320 seconds).

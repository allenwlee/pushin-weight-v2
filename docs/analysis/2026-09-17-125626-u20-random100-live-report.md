# U20 random 100 live translation and commentary run

**Run directory:** `docs/analysis/2026-09-17-125626-u20-random100-live-evidence/`  
**Status:** completed inference and reconciled automated semantic screening; quality gate remains open.

## Cohort

The cohort was sampled in one read-only, timeout-bounded transaction from
`pushinweight-db-shadow` (`dpg-d9koekqjobas73fvjqng-a`). The snapshot time was
2026-09-17T03:56:26.424322Z and the eligible window was the preceding 24 hours.
Selection used only `posts.fetched_at` and `ORDER BY random() LIMIT 100`; it did
not filter by language, length, role, or published time. `fetched_at` is the
first-ingest timestamp in the production model.

The eligible pool contained 3,960 posts. Its language distribution was en
2,977, zh-Hans 421, other 376, ja 152, zh-Hant 14, ko 18, zh 1, and tl 1. The
selected cohort contained en 69, other 13, zh-Hans 10, ja 6, zh-Hant 1, and ko
1. The [selection JSON](2026-09-17-125626-u20-random100-live-selection.json)
and the copied evidence directory retain every source row, original language
fields, timestamps, IDs, and available stored parent/quote context.

## Frozen execution contract

The run used the current per-post literal translator with raw v12 for single
lines and opt-in v13 line tracking and the current 4,000-character synthesis guard. Its route was
OpenRouter to DeepInfra (`deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731`, fp8, reasoning
disabled, temperature 1, top-p 1, seed 42). Maximum global provider concurrency
was three; calls within each translated post remained serial. The frozen request
set had 215 translation calls and 96 commentary calls, 311 total, with zero
transport retries and a maximum reservation of $1.64891628 under the $5 outer
cap.

Four sampled posts were retained but could not reach the commentary provider
because the current synthesis caller rejected their inputs at its existing
4,000-character cap: 2100074485743304992, 2100077710332584287,
2100079736537923768, and 2100320389226225806.

## Mechanical outcome

Translation attempted all 215 frozen requests: 202 responses were received and
13 OpenRouter 429 rate-limit failures were retained without replay. Twelve of 100 post
rows had at least one failed translation. The 85 native locale copies were
byte-exact. Two received responses were rejected by the current caller: post
2100277491390951481 returned its Indonesian source unchanged for Japanese, and
post 2100334057309847751 returned list-like marker/source pairs instead of the
required v13 marker blocks for Japanese.

Commentary attempted all 96 frozen provider requests: 88 were received and
eight provider-accepted responses were rejected by the adapter as invalid
content/JSON. One additional received commentary response failed the synthesis
schema check. Thirteen of 100 post rows lacked a complete commentary result:
the four pre-call cap rejections plus nine rows with provider/adapter/schema
failures. These counts are structural outcomes, not semantic quality judgments.

Provider-reported translation usage was 95,656 input tokens, 44,933 output
tokens, and $0.01343562, though the total is incomplete because failed calls
did not all return usage. Commentary reported 26,747 input tokens, 47,392
output tokens, and $0.01013538 with complete billing data. The known reported
total is $0.02357100. Mean/max measured call latency was 6,649/108,982 ms for
translation and 13,626/47,652 ms for commentary. End-to-end elapsed time was
931,320 ms (15 minutes 31.320 seconds).

## Evidence and semantic review

`contract.json`, frozen request hashes, raw provider responses, per-request
usage/latency/error receipts, staged translation result, commentary partials,
and the final result are preserved in the evidence directory. No database row,
runtime configuration, prompt, or production service was changed.

Independent reviewers screened every available output against source and stored
context, followed by parent reconciliation and separate independent checks of
disputed cases. Original judgments remain immutable alongside corrected views.
These are automated screening findings, not human gold or a proven population
error rate. Six translation outputs and three commentary outputs remain uncertain
and are not counted as either confirmed errors or clean successes.

| Measure | Translation | Commentary |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sampled source posts | 100 | 100 |
| Required generated outputs / locale strings | 215 | 300 (100 calls planned conceptually, each returns 3 locales) |
| Available generated outputs | 200 | 261 (87 complete posts) |
| Confirmed material defects after automated reconciliation | 7/200 (3.5%) | 15/261 (5.7%), affecting 5/87 complete posts |
| Uncertain outputs | 6 | 3 |
| Minor-only defects | 3 | 6 |
| Posts with missing outputs | 12/100 | 13/100 |
| Posts with either missing output or confirmed material defect | 17/100 | 18/100 |

Translation's 7/200 is a confirmed-screening fraction of all available outputs,
not a claim that every remaining output is correct; excluding six uncertain
outputs would make it 7/194 (3.6%). Commentary's analogous rate excluding three
uncertain outputs is 15/258 (5.8%). Multiple locale outputs from one post are
correlated. This uniformly selected sample describes this 24-hour collected
population, whose language mix differs from the older balanced 45-post corpus;
changes in rates do not isolate prompt improvements.

Translation defects: four untranslated outputs (French→Chinese, English→Japanese,
Indonesian→Chinese, Korean→Japanese); one Chinese discount mistranslated into
Japanese; one pair of model names translated into ordinary Chinese descriptors;
one Korean headline direction reversed in English. A reviewer initially flagged
the Chinese version of that headline incorrectly; reconciliation corrected it
and separately flagged the English version. A Japanese omitted-subject case was
moved to uncertain rather than treated as confirmed actor reassignment.

Commentary defects: unsupported nonexistence claims for named GPTs, unsupported
assertions that a supplied link is untrustworthy, an invented prior-commitments
explanation for service capacity, a free-pricing claim extended to other models,
and tokenizer evidence reinterpreted as a different-tokenizer architecture claim.
Each affects all three locale commentaries for its source post. Two groups of
initial flags were dismissed because they were reasonable, grounded commentary
inferences. The parent retained the capacity-cause flag despite one independent
reviewer's more permissive assessment; the evidence and disagreement are saved.

The current translator and commentary gates remain open. This study added no
second semantic call to runtime, no automatic repair, and no mandatory owner
review. Inference billing excludes the separate development/review-agent work.

What changed

  • Reviewed source assets in six-item batches instead of three.
  • Accepted two attributed source videos as possible still and clip sources; this avoided reviewing 32 additional XHS images for this case.
  • Deferred creator accreditation until the final media was chosen. Both original creators remain unestablished; source publisher and URL are retained, with no invented creator.
  • Checked saved frames with both the vision model and a local face top-margin measurement. This rejected a sharper close-up whose hair was cut by the source frame, then excluded that clip and its derived stills from repair.
  • Kept the real 7.96-second encoded clip when its container-reported end exceeded the last frame by 0.04 seconds.
Quality limit: All three final references come from compressed, older stage footage. They have intact head framing, but are less sharp than the rejected close-up.

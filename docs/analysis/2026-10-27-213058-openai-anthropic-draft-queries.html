OpenAI and Anthropic draft queries

Draft search texts for adding OpenAI and Anthropic as tracked brands, built from the keyword list mined from 116,378 of our posts. Nothing here is in any config yet. Each lab gets its own list search (combining lists in one search returned HTTP 400 today), its own @-mention search, and two or three keyword searches. The 9 existing searches stay unchanged.

Rules used: names with clear meaning are searched on their own; ambiguous words only count next to an AI word (AI model models llm api agent coding 模型 モデル); multi-word names are quoted so the search and the brand matcher agree. Claude and Codex are searched on their own, as agreed, with exclusion words applied after fetch. Every request also appends the 44-character time window, counted in the headroom below.

Checked on X on 2026-10-27 with direct searches: GPT matches hyphenated forms (GPT-5.6, GPT-6.1, so GPT-5.6 Sol is covered) but not joined ones (gpt6.1, GPT6.1Terra), so joined spellings are listed separately. Anthropic does not match AnthropicAI or @AnthropicAI. OpenAI matches OpenAIの next to Japanese, but our brand matcher does not yet. Bare Sol, Luna, Terra and Astra mostly mean OpenAI models in our posts and need an AI word nearby; Cyber was dropped because it almost always means cybersecurity. Still open: the volume that GPT, ChatGPT, Claude and Codex bring in with min_faves:0.

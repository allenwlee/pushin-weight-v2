How it works

## Four steps, and then it runs without you.

The whole product is one loop: monitor, discover, classify, act.

1. 01

Add a keyword

Your brand, your domain, your handle, a competitor. Aliases catch the spellings people actually use.

2. 02

We watch

Five networks are swept every four hours and deduplicated into one stream.

3. 03

AI filters

Each mention gets a category, a sentiment, an urgency score and a one-sentence summary.

4. 04

You act

Reply, alert the team, or let your own agent read the feed over MCP.

FAQ

## Questions people actually ask.

Do people have to tag my account?No. We search by keyword and alias, so a post that says your product name without linking or tagging you is found the same way.

How is this different from a search alert?Search alerts hand you every match. We classify each one — bug report, complaint, misinformation, product idea, testimonial — and score urgency, so you read ten mentions instead of four hundred.

Which sources are covered?X, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn and Threads. Podcasts are next. We would rather ship five sources that work than ten that half-work.

Can an AI agent use this?That is the point. Everything the dashboard does exists as a REST endpoint and an MCP tool of the same name, because they call the same function.

What happens when I hit my limit?Collection pauses and we tell you. There is no surprise overage bill — a hard ceiling protects you, and it protects our margins too.

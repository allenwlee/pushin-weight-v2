Misinformation


The problem

## Why this keeps getting missed.

A wrong statement about your product — “they sell your data”, “they shut down last year” — does not announce itself. It gets repeated, gains confidence, and eventually turns up in a sales call.

Plays

## What people actually do with it.

- Separate wrong from angry

Misinformation is its own category, distinct from a complaint. A complaint needs support; a false claim needs a correction.

- Act while reach is low

Engagement numbers travel with each mention, so you can tell a dead thread from one that is climbing.

- Keep an audit trail

Statuses — new, reviewed, done, ignored — record who handled what, without a separate spreadsheet.

FAQ

## Before you ask.

What counts as misinformation here?A factual claim about your product that appears to be untrue — pricing, security posture, ownership, availability. The model flags candidates; a human decides.

Can I be alerted only for this category?Yes. An alert rule can filter by a single category and a minimum urgency.

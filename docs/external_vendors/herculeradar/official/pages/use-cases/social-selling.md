The problem

## Why this keeps getting missed.

Outbound interrupts people who were not thinking about the problem. Recommendation threads are the opposite: a stranger has publicly asked to be sold to, and the window is measured in hours.

Plays

## What people actually do with it.

- Watch the alternative queries

Add “alternative to <competitor>” and “looking for <category>” as their own monitor. They behave nothing like brand keywords.

- Answer with substance, not a pitch

The mention includes the full thread link, so you can read the context before replying.

- Alert to the phone, not the dashboard

An email alert on high-urgency recommendation threads beats checking a feed twice a day.

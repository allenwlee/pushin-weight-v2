## Why this keeps getting missed.

Feedback boards capture the opinions of users motivated enough to sign in and type. Everyone else says “I wish it had X” in a thread you will never see, and that sentence is worth more than a survey.

Plays

## What people actually do with it.

- Find the integration everyone assumes you have

Product-idea mentions cluster fast. Three people asking for the same payment provider in a week is a roadmap signal.

- Hear the objection before the sales call

Public complaints about a competitor tell you which gap to close and which line to put on the pricing page.

- Quote real users in your changelog

Ship the request, then link back to the person who asked. It costs nothing and it converts.

FAQ

## Before you ask.

Does it distinguish a feature request from a complaint?Yes. Every mention gets one of five categories — bug report, complaint, misinformation, product idea or testimonial — plus a one-sentence summary of what the author actually wants.

Can my agent read these?That is the point of the MCP server. Connect Claude or ChatGPT and ask it to summarise this month’s product ideas directly.

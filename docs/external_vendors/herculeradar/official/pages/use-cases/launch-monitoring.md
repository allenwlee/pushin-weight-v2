The problem

## Why this keeps getting missed.

On launch day everyone refreshes a different tab, the same thread gets sent to the group chat four times, and the one comment that spotted a real bug is buried under congratulations. Afterwards nobody can say what actually landed.

Plays

## What people actually do with it.

- Raise the cadence for the day

A monitor that keeps finding new mentions is scanned more often automatically. Launch day is exactly when that pays.

- Split the celebration from the signal

Two hundred "congrats" posts and three bug reports arrive together. Category and urgency separate them without a human triaging.

- Write the retro from data

Filter by category over the launch window and the retro is a query, not a memory exercise.

FAQ

## Before you ask.

How quickly does a mention appear?Active monitors are swept every four hours, and a mention is classified within a few minutes of collection. It is fast enough to reply while a thread is still live, not fast enough to be a live feed.

Can I get launch-day alerts on my phone?Set an alert to a webhook and point it at whatever already reaches your phone. Email works too, but a chat webhook is what most teams use on the day.

FAQ

## Pricing questions.

What counts as a mention?One collected post. If a single post matches three of your aliases, or shows up in two consecutive scans, it counts once — deduplication happens before billing, not after.

Is there a free plan?There is a free trial of 100 mentions in total, not a recurring free tier. Collecting social data costs us real money on every scan, so an unlimited free plan would be dishonest pricing.

What happens at the limit?Collection pauses and the dashboard tells you. No overage charges appear on your card without you choosing an upgrade.

Do API and MCP cost extra?No. Every plan includes the REST API, the MCP server and webhooks. Only the per-minute rate limit differs.

Can I cancel?Any time, from the billing portal. The plan stays active until the end of the period.

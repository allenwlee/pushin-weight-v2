How we read it

## The mechanism, stated plainly.

Keyword search runs through a managed Actor that needs no cookies and no LinkedIn account — we never hold your credentials. Posts keep their author, company, reactions, comments and reposts.

Coverage

## What is collected — and what is not.

- Public post text
- Author name and profile
- Reactions, comments and reposts
- Company pages

- Comments on posts are not collected in the MVP — posts only.
- Anything behind a login, a private group or a connection-only audience is never touched.

FAQ

## Source-specific questions.

Do I need to connect my LinkedIn account?No. Retrieval happens on our side without cookies or a login, so there is no account to put at risk and nothing to reconnect when a session expires.

Can I track a competitor’s company page?Yes — add their name and page slug as keywords on a monitor in the Competitors group.

# Updates to our Terms and Privacy Policy

We're updating the X Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to support our evolving business, meet new legal requirements, and better reflect how we describe our products, features, and services.

Below is a summary of the primary changes that go into effect on January 15, 2026. You may also see a notification about these updates when you use X.

**Governing law and disputes:** We've updated our Terms to clarify that the choice of law and courts we use to resolve disputes depends on where you live, and to add an appeals process for certain decisions for people in the European Union and the United Kingdom.

**Our enforcement provisions:** We've updated our Terms to explain that in some places, for example, the EU and UK, we may need to enforce against additional categories of content that local law deems harmful or unsafe.

**User content responsibilities and restrictions:** We've updated our Terms to better reflect that you are responsible for the content you create on X — including any inputs, prompts, outputs, and information you obtain or create through our services.

**Age assurance considerations:** Our updated Privacy Policy explains that we may collect and share information to estimate or verify your age as required under law.

**Description of our services:** We've updated our Terms to better reflect how we describe our products, features, and services.

We encourage you to read these changes in full since they govern your use of X and how we handle your data. If you continue to use X after January 15, 2026, the updated Terms and Privacy Policy apply to you.

If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us [here](https://help.x.com/forms/privacy).
